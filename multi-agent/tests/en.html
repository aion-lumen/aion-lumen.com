DEVELOPMENT

How the lab grew.

  1. August–September 2026

    It started with mail.

    We first compared local models on email. Some returned empty or unreadable answers. Response quality, speed and manual checks guided the selection.

  2. 22 September

    Then came financial questions.

    Ledger taught us to test question understanding and calculations separately. After improvements, the workflow reached the results shown above.

  3. 24 September

    Short messages, closer inspection.

    Three models tried two instructions. We discovered that our expected answer for a meeting note was wrong and rescored the saved responses; the charts now show those results.

  4. Today

    A role for each model.

    Response quality, speed and manual checks guide the choice of mail models. The catalogue brings together twelve candidates from separate experiments; the two Qwen versions belong to the same family.

A useful next step: try Gemma 4 26B on more mail edge cases and tool calls.
